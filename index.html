How the Winner column is decided

  • Looks follow your ranking of all five: Jetour T1, then Tiggo 7 CSH Ultra, Jaecoo J7, J5 EV, Sealion 6.
  • Lower price wins (your dealer quotes where you have them; otherwise official SA list incl. VAT). The Tiggo 7 figure is your all-in (‘all up’) quote; the J7 and Sealion quotes are not labelled all-in, so a small rand gap is not automatically a like-for-like difference.
  • Higher power, torque, battery size, claimed or tested range, clearance, boot, towing, charging power, crash scores and warranty/service duration win.
  • Shorter, narrower and lower win on size, because garage fit next to the CX-5 matters. Longer wheelbase wins as cabin space for Sacha and granny.
  • Lower real-world fuel use wins among independently tested figures. A formal CAR road-test route and a CAR long-term daily average are both genuine tests, but they are not the same test; the lowest formal road-test figure still takes the win, with the long-term figure shown alongside. Claimed NEDC “1.x L/100 km with a charged battery” figures are not treated as equivalent to a road test.
  • Where a winner is not meaningful, or claimed and tested figures are too close, the Winner column says so and no cell is highlighted.
  • Tied winners are all named and all highlighted. Cells marked not stated, unconfirmed or dealer / review figure are not guessed.
  • Electric-range cycles are named: NEDC reads higher than WLTP or WLTC, so PHEV NEDC or ECE figures are not ranked against the Sealion 6's WLTP or the J5's WLTC as if they were the same test.
  • The Tiggo 7 column is always the CSH Ultra PHEV. Plus-trim equipment is mentioned only as a warning, never as this column’s figure.
